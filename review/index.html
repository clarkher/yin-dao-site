Sound Duel — App Review walkthrough

Supplied for Guideline 2.1, Information Needed. App name in Traditional Chinese: 聲道對決.

Direct link to the video file

What the recording shows

  1. The app launching for the first time, on a freshly erased device.
  2. The first-run guide, which offers to walk the player through each of the four events one at a time.
  3. The main screen: the four events as tiles, each with the player's own best result, and the overall rank badge.
  4. The microphone explanation screen and the system microphone prompt.
  5. All four scoring events played through to a result: Sustain, Pitch, High Note and Volume, each with its live readout, its rank, and the button to its Game Center leaderboard.
  6. Pass-and-play: entering player names, choosing the Humming Quiz mode, and a complete four-question round with the final standings.
  7. The about screen, which links to the privacy policy, the terms and contact.
Where the sound in this recording comes from. The video was recorded in the iOS Simulator on a Mac that has no microphone, so the audio the app hears is a synthesized test tone fed in through a virtual audio device. The app is unmodified: the same release code path reads the microphone input and computes every number you see. The tone is a slowly sweeping note rather than a human voice, which is why the Pitch score in the video is poor — that is the test tone, not the app.

How to reach every feature

  • No account, no login, no sample data and no demo credentials. Every feature is available immediately after installation.
  • Four events — tap any tile on the main screen. Sustain (how long one breath lasts), Pitch (how closely you match a played reference tone), High Note (how high you can hold a note) and Volume.
  • Global leaderboards — the button appears on the result screen after a round. Game Center sign-in is optional; nothing else depends on it.
  • Pass-and-play — the wide button at the bottom of the main screen. Two to four players on one phone, either competing on one of the four events or playing the Humming Quiz.
  • About — the link under the pass-and-play button, with the privacy policy and terms.

Audio handling

Audio is analysed live on the device and discarded frame by frame. Nothing is recorded, written to disk or sent anywhere. The app works in airplane mode. The only network feature is the optional Game Center leaderboard.

The Volume event reports a self-defined 0–100 scale, not a decibel value. A phone microphone is not calibrated, so the app does not print a sound pressure level, and both the app and the store description say so. The Sustain event is a game about holding one breath; the app states that it is not a measurement of anybody's body or health, caps a round at 60 seconds and asks the player to rest after three rounds in a row.
